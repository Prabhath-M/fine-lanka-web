## Selection Notes

The Kandyan dancer and Kandy Esala Perahera file pages were checked directly on Wikimedia Commons. Both pages identify Creative Commons licences. The higher-resolution **Kandy Dance (6493011161).jpg** photograph was selected in place of the small initial Kandyan dance candidate. The Arugam Bay Commons category was also reviewed; **Arugam Waves (238816201).jpeg** was confirmed as an Arugam Bay photograph under CC BY 3.0. The exact public files are copied directly into the local `public/images` directory for packaging.
